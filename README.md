## Other Documents

1. Master Services Agreement (MSA)

   •	Purpose: An overarching contract that outlines the general terms and conditions governing the overall relationship between you and your client.
   •	Use Case: When you have an ongoing relationship with a client involving multiple projects or services, the MSA sets the foundational terms, while individual SOWs specify project-specific details.

2. Change Order Form

   •	Purpose: To document any changes to the original Scope of Work, including adjustments to deliverables, timelines, and costs.
   •	Use Case: When clients request additions or modifications during the project, a Change Order Form ensures both parties agree to the new terms in writing.

3. Service Level Agreement (SLA)

   •	Purpose: Defines the level of service you will provide, including uptime guarantees, response times, and support availability.
   •	Use Case: Particularly important if you’re offering ongoing support, maintenance, or hosting services.

4. Maintenance and Support Agreement

   •	Purpose: Outlines the terms under which you will provide post-launch support and maintenance, including scope, fees, and duration.
   •	Use Case: For clients who require ongoing updates, security patches, or technical support after the project completion.

5. Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

   •	Purpose: Ensures compliance with data protection laws like GDPR when processing personal data on behalf of your clients.
   •	Use Case: Necessary if you’ll handle, store, or process personal data as part of your services.

6. Privacy Policy

   •	Purpose: Discloses how you collect, use, store, and protect personal information.
   •	Use Case: If your work involves collecting data from users (e.g., through websites or apps), a Privacy Policy is legally required in many jurisdictions.

7. Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement

   •	Purpose: Clarifies the transfer of intellectual property rights from you to the client upon completion and payment.
   •	Use Case: When projects involve significant IP creation, and you want to formalize the transfer beyond what’s in the Ts&Cs.

8. Acceptance Certificate

   •	Purpose: A document the client signs to confirm that the deliverables meet the agreed-upon acceptance criteria.
   •	Use Case: Provides clear closure for project milestones or final delivery, reducing disputes over whether work is complete.

9. Invoice Template

   •	Purpose: Standardizes your billing process with all necessary details for payment.
   •	Use Case: Ensures you include all required information for accounting purposes and facilitates timely payments.

10. Project Proposal

    •	Purpose: Outlines your understanding of the client’s needs, proposed solutions, timelines, and costs before formal agreements are made.
    •	Use Case: Used during the sales process to define the project’s potential scope and value, which can later be formalized in the SOW.

11. Non-Compete and Non-Solicitation Agreements

    •	Purpose: Prevents clients or subcontractors from engaging in activities that directly compete with your business or soliciting your employees.
    •	Use Case: When you want to protect your business interests, especially in close partnerships or when sharing sensitive information.

12. Subcontractor Agreement

    •	Purpose: Outlines the terms and expectations when hiring third parties to perform parts of the work.
    •	Use Case: Ensures that subcontractors are bound by the same confidentiality, quality standards, and IP provisions as you are with your client.

13. Risk Assessment Document

    •	Purpose: Identifies potential risks in the project and outlines mitigation strategies.
    •	Use Case: For complex projects where risk management is critical to success.

14. Client Onboarding Checklist

    •	Purpose: Standardizes the process of gathering necessary information and resources from new clients.
    •	Use Case: Ensures you have all the initial details required to start the project efficiently.

15. Project Closure Document

    •	Purpose: Summarizes the completed work, gathers client feedback, and officially closes the project.
    •	Use Case: Provides an opportunity for reflection, captures lessons learned, and can be useful for future projects or case studies.

16. License Agreements

    •	Purpose: Specifies terms under which the client can use software, code, or other IP that you retain ownership of.
    •	Use Case: When delivering products that include proprietary code or when you’re providing software as a service (SaaS).

17. Insurance Certificates

    •	Purpose: Demonstrates that you have professional liability or errors and omissions insurance.
    •	Use Case: Some clients may require proof of insurance before entering into a contract.
